Affine texture mapping interpolates texture coordinates linearly in screen space.
However, this bends textures on surfaces that recede from the viewer.
We derive the largest error along an edge in closed form.
As a fraction of the edge, it depends only on the ratio of the depths of the endpoints.
From it, we bound the error of subdivided surfaces and compute how finely to subdivide them.
For a floor whose far edge is four times as distant as its near edge, 24 by 24 cells reduce the largest error from 121 to 0.44 texels.
Measurements with a software rasterizer stay within \qty{0.1}{\percent} of our predictions.
